Privacy Policy

King's Counsel Chess · Last updated October 10, 2026

The short version: King's Counsel Chess has no accounts and no servers that store your data. Your games, settings and progress stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; for anyone under 18 those ads are never personalized. Players 13+ can type chess questions to the coach — the question and board position are sent to answer it and nothing is stored. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Who we are

King's Counsel Chess is a chess app with a live coach, for kids and adults. You can reach us at sagarika125@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your age band, optional first name, games, coach notes, settings, puzzles progress and everything you do in the app are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

We ask for an age band for two reasons: to pick the right coach (Hopper for young kids, the Vizier for teens and adults) and to decide what kind of ads you may see (see below). It is not sent to us.

Typed questions to the coach (ages 13+ only)

Players aged 13 and older can type a chess question during a game. The question, the current board position and engine facts are sent to our server (Cloudflare) so an AI model can answer in a few sentences about chess only. Nothing is stored — not the question, not the position, not the answer. A random install code is sent with the question only to limit how many each device can ask per day. Players under 13 never see this feature.

Ads (free version only)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are never shown during a game.

  • Under 13: child-directed treatment — ads are never personalized and the app never asks for tracking permission.
  • 13–17: non-personalized ads; the app never asks for tracking permission.
  • 18+: Google's consent form is shown where required, then Apple's tracking prompt. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized.

Google's ad SDK may collect a device identifier, coarse location, and ad interaction data as described in Google's data disclosure list.

Purchases

King's Counsel Pro (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) is sold and processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.

Children

Kids play with Hopper's fully offline coaching: no typed chat, no accounts, no data sent anywhere. Because we don't collect personal information from children, we have none to review or delete. A parent can erase all progress at any time by deleting the app. Questions? Email sagarika125@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
